Session end

Practice ends at

The voice warns 30, 20 and 10 minutes before. When the time comes, the round being flown is finished first (every heat in it gets its turn), then practice stops and everyone stays signed in. In free flight, or before a round has started, it stops right away. The end time is for this session only: it's cleared when practice stops.

Club quads

For pilots without their own quad. At the kiosk they tap I don't have my own quad and get the first free club quad that isn't broken, on its channel. When every working one is taken, the one with the fewest pilots is shared: each of them flies a different heat, on that quad's channel. tgiFlyDay remembers who flew a club quad and offers it again next time.

Marking a quad Broken moves its pilots to another club quad straight away, and the voice tells them to check the big screen. Up to 8 club quads.

Video

What it will do

Each pilot's FPV feed plays in their plate on the big screen while they fly, and every heat is recorded, so you can watch any heat again from the Times page, lined up with the laps.

Not built yet. It needs the capture hardware first: one USB grabber per receiver, or a quad splitter into one capture card. Once it's here, its settings (which input is which channel, recording on or off, where recordings go) live on this page.

Spectrum analyser

What it will do

Listens to the 5.8 GHz band and shows which channels are noisy, so tgiFlyDay can steer pilots away from them when it hands out channels.

Not built yet. It runs on the Waveshare ESP32-C5 with a receiver module, cabled to this PC. Its settings (which port, how often it scans) will live on this page.
