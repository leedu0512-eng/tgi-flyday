Line up with the hall

The phone needs two points on the floor to know where the hall is. Stand on the carpet, point the phone at the floor and tap:

How it works

  1. Move the phone slowly over the floor until the ring sits on it.
  2. Tap the two points. The carpet's outline then shows where the phone thinks it is: check it lies on the real edges.
  3. Every piece stands where it goes. The one up next is in bright colours, with a green post. Put it there and tap Placed.
  4. Far from where you lined up, if things look off, line up again with nearer points.
